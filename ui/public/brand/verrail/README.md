# Verrail Brand Assets

## Direction

The selected **Evidence Rail** mark combines two converging rails with a square inspection tie. It represents governed forward motion: work advances through explicit evidence rather than through an abstract AI sparkle or bot symbol.

## Palette

| Token | Value | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#13221E` | Primary mark and wordmark on light surfaces |
| Pine | `#16705F` | Identity accent |
| Deep Pine | `#0F4F45` | App-icon field |
| Signal Mint | `#70D6BF` | Mark accent on dark surfaces |
| Frost | `#F6FFFC` | Mark and wordmark on dark surfaces |

No gradient is part of the identity system. `mark-mono.svg` is the authority for one-color reproduction.

## Files

- `mark-mono.svg`: one-color reproduction master.
- `mark-dark.svg` and `mark-light.svg`: mark for light and dark surfaces.
- `wordmark-dark.svg` and `wordmark-light.svg`: wordmark-only assets.
- `lockup-dark.svg` and `lockup-light.svg`: horizontal product lockups.
- `favicon.svg`: square browser source.
- `app-icon.svg`: full-bleed app-icon source.
- `app-icon-maskable.svg`: safe-zone source for maskable PWA icons.
- `icon-*.png`: generated raster delivery sizes.
- `asset-manifest.json`: dimensions and SHA-256 hashes verified by `pnpm check:brand-assets`.

## Usage

- Minimum mark size: 16 px digital, 5 mm print.
- Minimum horizontal lockup width: 104 px digital, 28 mm print.
- Clear space: keep at least one checkpoint-bar height around the mark or lockup.
- Use the dark assets on light, low-noise surfaces and the light assets on dark, solid surfaces.
- Do not rotate, outline, add shadows, change the rail/checkpoint geometry, place the mark in a decorative container, or recolor individual geometry outside the documented palette.
- The SVG wordmarks use the product font stack `Inter, Arial, sans-serif`; final public-launch artwork should convert the approved wordmark to outlines after optical refinement.

These assets are the B0 product baseline. They are not evidence of trademark clearance.
