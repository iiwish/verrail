# Verrail Brand Concepts

## Brand Problem

Verrail needs to signal controlled forward motion: AI work advances, but only through inspectable evidence, explicit gates, and durable execution. The mark must remain recognizable at 16-24 px, work in one color, and avoid the generic AI vocabulary of sparkles, neural-node webs, chat bubbles, and letterforms inside circles.

## Explored Directions

### 1. Evidence Rail

Two converging rails form a strong `V`; a square cross-tie acts as the evidence checkpoint. It combines motion and governance without relying on a checkmark. The silhouette is compact, symmetric, and survives monochrome reduction.

### 2. Version Gate

Stacked offset frames represent versioned artifacts passing through a release gate. The idea communicates version control well, but at favicon scale it reads too much like generic layers or documents.

### 3. Control Switch

A branching path resolves into one authorized line. It expresses decisions and approvals, but the branch geometry becomes too diagram-like and resembles workflow products already common in the category.

### 4. Proof Seal

Four corner brackets close around a stable center. It communicates inspection, but resembles scanning and computer-vision products more than governed delivery.

## Selected Direction

**Evidence Rail** is selected for B0. Its open top expresses work entering the system, its converging rails express controlled progress, and the cross-tie is a discrete inspection gate. It is not a literal paperclip, bot, or generic network, so it supports the domain transition rather than decorating the inherited product.

## Refinement Notes

- Keep the mark filled and geometric rather than stroke-dependent.
- Preserve a square checkpoint; rounding weakens the control-plane character.
- At 16 px, remove no elements: the rail/checkpoint relationship must remain the same at every size.
- Use the Pine color only as an identity accent. The mark must always have a valid one-color rendering.
- Treat this as the launch-direction asset system, subject to professional trademark clearance and final optical refinement before a broad public launch.
